Frequently Asked Questions

A simple public guide to help voters understand the Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls in Telangana, what to expect during BLO visits, and what to do if their name is missing or marked for verification.

Important Dates

Telangana SIR timeline

15–24 Jun 2026

Preparation & Training

Officials prepare, train, and print materials before the field exercise begins.

25 Jun–24 Jul 2026

House-to-House Enumeration

BLOs visit homes and collect/verify enumeration forms.

31 Jul 2026

Draft Electoral Roll

Draft roll is published. This is the key point to check if your name is included.

31 Jul–30 Aug 2026

Claims & Objections

Use this window to correct errors, respond to notices, or claim inclusion if eligible.

31 Jul–28 Sep 2026

Notice & Disposal Phase

Officials dispose of claims, objections, notices, and verification cases.

01 Oct 2026

Final Electoral Roll

The final roll for this SIR schedule is published.

Quick Action Checklist

What should every voter do now?

1

Check your current voter details

Check whether your name, address, polling part, and EPIC details are correctly shown on the voter portal or official CEO/ECI sources.

2

Be available during BLO house visits

During the house-to-house phase, cooperate with the BLO and ensure your enumeration form is filled and submitted.

3

Find your 2002 link if possible

For Telangana, the relevant previous intensive revision date is 01.01.2002. You may link yourself or a relative to the last SIR roll where applicable.

4

Keep documents ready, but do not panic

No document is to be collected from electors during the Enumeration Phase. Documents may become relevant later if notice is issued or details do not match.

5

Check the draft roll on 31 July

If your name is missing, wrongly marked, or has errors, act during the claims and objections window.

FAQs

Simple answers for voters and volunteers

What is SIR?

SIR means Special Intensive Revision of the electoral roll. It is a special exercise by the Election Commission to verify, update, correct, include, or remove entries in the voter list according to law.

Is Telangana included in this SIR phase?

Yes. Telangana is included in the phase mentioned in the ECI instruction dated 14 May 2026. For Telangana, the qualifying date shown in the schedule is 01.10.2026.

When will BLOs visit houses in Telangana?

As per the schedule, house-to-house visits by BLOs in Telangana are from 25 June 2026 to 24 July 2026.

What is an Enumeration Form?

The Enumeration Form is used during SIR to verify details of existing electors. It can include the elector’s name, EPIC, address, current photo, date of birth, optional Aadhaar number, mobile number, family details, and previous SIR roll linkage details.

Will documents be collected during the Enumeration Phase?

No. The ECI instruction specifically says that no document is to be collected from electors during the Enumeration Phase. Keep documents ready for later verification only if needed.

Is Aadhaar compulsory?

The Enumeration Form mentions Aadhaar as optional. The ECI instruction also refers to separate Aadhaar-related directions. Do not assume that Aadhaar alone proves citizenship or that it is compulsory for every voter.

What is the “2002 voter list” link people are talking about?

For Telangana, the relevant last intensive revision date listed in the document is 01.01.2002. During SIR, a person may link themselves or a relative with entries in the electoral roll of the relevant last intensive revision, where applicable.

Where can I check previous SIR electoral roll details?

The information sheets in the document mention that electors/applicants can check their name and relevant relative’s name in previous SIR electoral rolls at the voter portal: voters.eci.gov.in. You may also ask the concerned BLO for help.

What happens if my previous SIR linkage is not found or does not match?

The ERO may issue a notice to electors/applicants whose previous SIR roll details are unavailable or do not match. After receiving a notice, the person may be asked to provide supporting documents according to the category applicable to them.

What happens if my Enumeration Form is not returned?

The BLO may identify a probable reason such as Absent, Shifted, Death, or Duplicate after inquiry from nearby electors. Booth-wise lists of such electors not included in the draft roll should be displayed locally and also made available through CEO/DEO websites.

Where will missing-name or non-inclusion lists be displayed?

The instruction mentions display at places such as Panchayat Bhavan, urban local body offices, Block Development Officer/Panchayat/Urban Local Body offices, and on CEO/DEO websites.

What should I do when the draft electoral roll is published?

Check your name immediately after the draft roll is published on 31 July 2026. If your name is missing, your details are wrong, or you receive a notice, use the claims and objections period from 31 July to 30 August 2026.

Can my name be deleted without notice?

The process includes notices, claims, objections, and inquiry. If your name is missing from the draft roll or you are marked for verification, respond quickly and keep proof ready. Do not wait until the final roll.

I am a new voter. What form do I need?

New voters generally use Form 6. During house-to-house enumeration, BLOs are directed to carry blank Form 6 and Declaration Forms to supply to anyone seeking enrolment as a new elector.

I shifted from another place. What should I do?

If you have shifted, you may need to use the appropriate correction/shifting process, commonly Form 8, along with the Declaration Form where applicable. Ask your BLO or check the official voter portal for the exact form for your case.

What documents may be asked later if notice is issued?

The document gives an indicative list, including birth certificate, passport, matriculation/educational certificate, permanent residence certificate, caste certificate, forest right certificate, government/PSU identity or pension documents, family register, and government land/house allotment certificate. The list is indicative and not exhaustive.

What documents are needed if I was born before 01.07.1987?

If notice is issued, the information sheet says a person born in India before 01.07.1987 may provide any listed document for self establishing date of birth and/or place of birth.

What if I was born between 01.07.1987 and 02.12.2004?

If notice is issued, the information sheet says to provide a listed document for self, and also a listed document for father or mother, establishing date/place of birth.

What if I was born after 02.12.2004?

If notice is issued, the information sheet says to provide a listed document for self, and documents for both father and mother. If any parent is not Indian, a copy of that parent’s valid passport and visa at the time of birth may be required.

What if someone was born outside India?

The information sheet says proof of birth registration issued by the Indian Mission abroad may be required. If citizenship was acquired by registration or naturalisation, the citizenship registration certificate may be required.

What should volunteers tell people first?

Tell them: check your voter details, cooperate during BLO visits, do not ignore notices, check the draft roll on 31 July, and file claims or corrections within the official window if needed.
