Know Your Rights

Voting in Telangana belongs to every eligible citizen.

Right to Register

Every citizen aged 18+ can enroll in the electoral roll and vote in local, state, and national elections.

Right to Information

Access voter details, polling station locations, and election updates through official EC Telangana channels.

Right to a Fair Vote

Use your EVM ballot confidentially and seek help from polling officials if you face any issues at the booth.

How to Participate

Simple steps to register and stay ready for the next election.

1

Check Eligibility

Be an Indian citizen residing in Telangana and at least 18 years old on election day.

2

Gather Documents

Use Aadhaar, passport, driving license, or other valid proof of identity and address.

3

Apply Online

Visit the Telangana CEO or ECI portal and complete Form 6 for voter registration.

4

Confirm Your Name

Verify your entry in the electoral roll and locate your polling station ahead of time.
